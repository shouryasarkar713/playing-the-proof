# Paper citations

- Arun Kumar Das and Tomáš Valla, “On Erdős–Szekeres Maker–Breaker
  games”, *Computing in Geometry and Topology* 5(2), Article 3, 2026.
  DOI: 10.57717/cgt.v5i2.73 (CC-BY 4.0).

Background and terminology:

- A. Džuklevski, D. Pálvölgyi, A. Pokrovskiy, C. D. Tóth, T. Valla and
  T. Verlinde, “Erdős–Szekeres Maker–Breaker Games”,
  arXiv:2505.15366v2 (2026). Monochromatic vs bichromatic terminology;
  the formal “k-strip” (Definition 2) — a different concept from this
  project's growth-strip visualization.
- O. Aichholzer et al., “Erdős–Szekeres-Type Games”, EuroCG 2019.
  Game taxonomy.

  h(6) = 30; the Overmars-29 coordinates are transcribed from their
  Fig. 8.
- B. Subercaseaux et al., “Formal Verification of the Empty Hexagon
  Number”, ITP 2024 (preprint arXiv:2403.17370v1). Lean formalization
  of h(6) = 30.
- P. Erdős and G. Szekeres (1935); P. Erdős (1978); H. Harborth
  (1978, H(5) = 10); J. D. Horton (1983); P. Kolipaka and
  S. Govindarajan (2013); D. Hefetz et al., *Positional Games*
  (Springer, 2014); T. Valla (2006, Master's thesis).
